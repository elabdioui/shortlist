# Recruiter Guide (English)

Welcome to **Shortlist**! This guide walks you through screening a batch of CVs in under 30 minutes, with zero technical jargon.

> **Important:** Shortlist helps you decide which CVs to read first, but **you always make the final hiring decision**. No candidate is ever rejected automatically.

## 1. Set Up Your AI Key

Before your first screening run, open **Settings → AI Providers**:
- Add an **AI key** (for example Google Gemini or Mistral), or use local **Ollama** if your organization enabled it.
- Click **Test key** to verify connectivity.
- You can add multiple keys: if one reaches its quota, Shortlist automatically switches to the next available key.

## 2. Create a Job & Build a Strong Criteria Grid

1. Click **New Job** and paste the job description (or upload a PDF/DOCX).
2. Click **Generate Criteria**: Shortlist proposes 5 to 12 criteria.
3. **Review and refine the grid:**
   - **Mandatory vs. Bonus:** A missing mandatory criterion routes the candidate to *Review* (or *Not shortlisted, to confirm* if explicitly contradicted).
   - **Weight (1–5):** Assign higher weights to must-have skills.
   - **Be measurable:** Prefer *"3+ years in industrial procurement"* over *"dynamic personality"*.
   - **Fairness:** Discriminatory criteria (age, gender, origin, photo, marital status) are automatically rejected.

## 3. Upload CVs & Run Analysis

1. Drag and drop **PDF, DOCX, TXT** files or a **ZIP archive**.
2. Shortlist automatically detects duplicate CVs and shows an **estimated cost and duration** before you start.
3. Start the analysis. You can watch live progress or close the tab—the analysis continues in the background.

## 4. Review the Ranking & Candidate Sheet

In the ranking table:
- **Interview:** High score (≥ 70 by default) and all mandatory criteria met.
- **Review:** Intermediate score or a mandatory criterion is missing/unproven.
- **Not shortlisted, to confirm:** Low score (< 45) or a mandatory criterion is contradicted. *Requires human confirmation.*
- **Manual review:** Unreadable file or hidden text detected.

Click any candidate to open their **detailed sheet**:
- Left: the **original CV**.
- Right: each criterion with its verdict (**Yes / Partial / No / Unproven**) and the **exact quote** verified in the CV.
- If you disagree with a verdict, override it directly—the score recalculates immediately.
- Record your decision (**Shortlisted / Rejected / On hold**) and add a note.

## 5. Export Results

From the ranking table, click **Export Excel (.xlsx)** or **Export CSV** to share the shortlist, decisions, and evidence with hiring managers.
